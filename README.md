# Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes coffee shop sales data to provide insights into revenue, customer purchasing patterns, and product performance. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor business performance and identify trends for data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

* Track overall sales performance.
* Analyze monthly and daily sales trends.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Monitor quantity sold and revenue generated.
* Support business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## Tools Used

* **Power BI Desktop**
* **Microsoft Excel** (Data Source)
* **Power Query** (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* **DAX** (Measures and Calculations)

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains transaction-level coffee shop sales data, including:

* Transaction Date
* Transaction Time
* Product Category
* Product Type
* Unit Price
* Quantity Sold
* Total Sales

## Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity Sold
* Average Sales
* Number of Transactions

### Visualizations

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Daily Sales Analysis
* Sales by Product Category
* Top-Selling Products
* Revenue Distribution Charts

## Key Insights

* Identifies peak sales periods.
* Highlights best-performing product categories.
* Tracks sales growth over time.
* Supports inventory and marketing decisions.

## Skills Demonstrated

* Data Cleaning and Transformation
* Data Modeling
* DAX Calculations
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence Reporting
* Dashboard Design

## How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
2. Refresh the dataset if required.
3. Interact with filters and slicers to explore insights.
4. Review KPIs and visualizations for business performance analysis.
